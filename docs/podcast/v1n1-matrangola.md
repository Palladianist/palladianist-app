## Instructions — English episode (max 8 minutes)

Two hosts discuss Viviana Matrangola's article "The Limit and the Poetics of Wonder. Heritage communities and the Faro Convention", for architecture and heritage students, researchers and educated listeners. Tone: rigorous and warm, never hype. Maximum 8 minutes.

Structure:
1. Hook: the limit as a value, not just a constraint, in a time of overtourism and commodification.
2. The four meanings of limit (material, legal, ethical, social), from the Latin limes.
3. The Faro Convention (2005): heritage communities and their principles.
4. Why dialogue between institutions and communities matters: cultural, social, economic, educational and environmental consequences.
5. The poetics of wonder, with examples from Apulia and the figure of Frederick II.
6. The Apulia Region's Care of Places project and the Coordination for the Care of Places (Resolution 655/2025).
7. Close: heritage in temporary custody for future generations (Hans Jonas).

Rules: say only what the article says; do not invent places, dates or data; make clear that panel (b) of Fig. 6 is the author's own proposal, not part of the regional resolution; pronounce Italian names correctly (Gargano, Valle d'Itria, Alberobello, Novoli, Castel del Monte).

6. Il progetto regionale Cura dei Luoghi e il Coordinamento per la Cura dei Luoghi (DGR 655/2025).
